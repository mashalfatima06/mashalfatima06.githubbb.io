How It Works

Live in days. ROI from Day One.

We handle everything. Our team builds, trains, integrates and deploys your AI - you just watch the appointments roll in.

01

Lead Captured from Website or Ad Form

A prospect submits a form on your website, Facebook ad, or landing page. The lead data is instantly sent to MashaAI Connect in real time.

02

AI Calls the Lead Within Seconds

Our AI agent places a call to the lead within 60 seconds - when their interest is highest. It introduces itself naturally and starts a qualifying conversation.

03

Qualifies & Handles Objections

The AI asks your custom qualifying questions, handles common objections intelligently, and determines if the prospect is a good fit for your offer.

04

Books Appointment into Your Calendar

Once qualified, the AI checks your live calendar availability, confirms a time with the prospect, and books the appointment - all in a single call.

05

Sends Reminders & Reduces No-Shows

Automated SMS and email reminders are sent before the appointment, dramatically reducing no-shows and cancellations.
